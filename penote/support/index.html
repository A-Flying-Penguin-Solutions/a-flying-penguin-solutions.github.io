Support

PeNote helps you find nearby products, organise wishlists, record pending and completed purchases, and coordinate selected shopping information with your household.

Contact PeNote Support Email kevin.lu@flyingpenguin.com.my for app issues, feedback, feature requests, privacy questions, or content reports.

How we can help

App and account

Get help signing in, synchronising shopping records, managing household access, or resolving an app error.

Purchases

Restore a one-time purchase, check access to paid features, or manage an Apple subscription.

Privacy and deletion

Learn how PeNote handles information or delete your account and associated cloud content.

Shared content

Report inaccurate, inappropriate, abusive, or rights-infringing public content.

Contacting support

To help us investigate, include the PeNote app version, your iOS or iPadOS version, device model, what you expected, and what happened. Screenshots are useful when they do not contain sensitive information.

Never email your password, payment-card details, or Apple Account password.

Account deletion

  1. Open PeNote and go to your account settings.
  2. Select Delete Account.
  3. Review the consequences and confirm the deletion request.

Deleting your account removes the account and its associated private, household, and publicly shared content from PeNote’s active systems. Protected backup copies may remain for up to 90 days. See the PeNote Privacy Policy for details.

Account deletion does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel an active subscription before deleting your account if you do not want it to renew.

Purchases and subscriptions

  • Use PeNote’s Restore Purchases action while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  • View or cancel Apple subscriptions.
  • Request a refund from Apple.

Apple handles billing, payment methods, subscription cancellation, and refund decisions. Contact us if PeNote does not recognise an entitlement after Apple confirms the purchase.

Location, camera, and photos

PeNote requests foreground location only when you ask for nearby results. Location is processed on your device. You can deny location access and choose an area manually. Camera and photo access is requested only when you scan a barcode or choose to add an image. Manage these permissions in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

Report or block shared content

Use the Report control on shared content to report material that is inaccurate, abusive, objectionable, or infringes your rights. Use the relevant user’s Block control to stop interactions. If you cannot access those controls, email support with enough information to locate the content. Do not forward harmful media unnecessarily.
