Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

Information and its use

Product details, barcodes, notes, store locations, prices, and preferences are saved on your device to provide the app’s features. The current version does not upload these records to our servers or share them with other users.

If you contact us, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond to your request.

Camera and location

Camera access lets you scan barcodes on your device. Optional precise location access lets you fill in a store’s coordinates using your current location; those coordinates are kept when you save the store. You can enter details manually and change permissions in your device settings.

Third-party services

Maps load from OpenStreetMap. Its servers receive your IP address and requests for the map area you view, as described in the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

Our email provider processes support messages. These services may process information outside your country. We may disclose information we hold when required by law.

We do not sell personal information or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Storage and deletion

Saved records remain in the app’s storage on your device. To remove them, delete the app and its data through your device settings. Any device backups are managed through your platform’s settings.

We keep support messages only as long as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent us.

Children’s privacy

PeNote is not directed specifically to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has sent us personal information without appropriate authorisation.

Policy updates

We will update this policy and its effective date when our features or privacy practices change.
