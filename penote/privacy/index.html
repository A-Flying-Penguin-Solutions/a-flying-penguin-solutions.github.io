Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information PeNote handles when you find products, manage shopping records, coordinate with your household, and share product information.

Effective date: 26 September 2026

In short PeNote uses your information to provide and secure the app. We do not sell personal information, show third-party advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Information we handle

  • Account information: your email address and the authentication information needed to create and secure your account.
  • Shopping and household content: products, stores, wishlists, pending and completed purchases, preferences, notes, household memberships, and content you choose to share with household members.
  • Public contributions: product or store information, prices, barcodes, photos, reviews, and other content you choose to publish for other users.
  • Purchase information: product identifiers, transaction identifiers, and entitlement or subscription status supplied by Apple. We do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics may process crash traces, app and operating-system versions, device model and state, timestamps, and installation identifiers so we can identify and fix reliability problems.
  • Support communications: your email address and any information you include when asking for help or reporting content.

Location, camera, photos, and barcodes

When you ask to find nearby products, PeNote may request your precise location while you are using the app. Your coordinates are used on your device to rank nearby results and are not sent to our servers or retained by us.

Camera and photo-library access is used only when you choose to scan a barcode, select an image, or take a product photo. Scanning is performed on your device. A barcode, photo, or related product record is uploaded only when you choose to save, share, or publish it through PeNote.

How we use information

We use information to provide accounts and synchronisation, save shopping records, enable household coordination and public contributions, manage purchase entitlements, answer support requests, prevent abuse, moderate shared content, maintain security, and diagnose technical problems.

How information is shared

  • Content marked for household sharing is available to members of that household.
  • Content you publish publicly can be viewed by other PeNote users.
  • Service providers may process information on our behalf for hosting, storage, authentication, security, support, and diagnostics.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics processes diagnostic information under Google’s applicable terms and privacy practices.
  • Apple processes App Store downloads, purchases, subscriptions, and refunds under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • We may disclose information when required by law, to protect people and the service, or in connection with a business reorganisation subject to appropriate safeguards.

Retention and account deletion

We keep account information and cloud content while your account is active or as needed to provide PeNote. You can initiate deletion from PeNote’s account settings. Account deletion removes your account and associated personal, household, and publicly shared content from active systems, except where retention is legally required.

Residual copies may remain in protected backups for up to 90 days before deletion. Firebase states that Crashlytics crash data and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days before removal begins. Apple may retain purchase records under its own legal and operational requirements.

Deleting PeNote does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple account before deleting your PeNote account.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your content in the app, choose whether eligible content is private, shared with your household, or public, control camera, photo, and location permissions in device settings, and delete your account from the app. You can also contact us to ask about your information or report content.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. PeNote and its service providers may process information in countries other than your own, where privacy laws may differ.

Children’s privacy

PeNote is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children under 13. A child who is not old enough to consent to data processing in their country should use PeNote only with permission and supervision from a parent or guardian. Contact us if you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate authorisation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PeNote’s features, providers, or privacy practices change. We will post the revised policy here with a new effective date.
